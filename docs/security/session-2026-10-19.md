## The number in the report is not PengyOS's exposure

The report's headline — 180 open, 68 P0/P1 — is measured against the **stock-Buildroot
baseline** (`tree = BUILDROOT` in the observation), not against what we ship.

Measured on the 2026-10-20 observation:

| tree | matches | open P0/P1 |
|---|---|---|
| BUILDROOT (what Buildroot ships) | 173 | **66** |
| PENGYOS (what we ship) | 24 | **0 triaged as P0/P1** |

Every P0/P1 match in the observation is a Buildroot-baseline match. That is not a
bug — the loop needs the baseline to know what a bump would buy — but it means the
headline number is not our exposure, and it will not move when we fork, because
forking does not change what Buildroot ships.

**Caveat on the PENGYOS column:** 7 of its 24 matches were untriaged when I measured
(ffmpeg-pengyos, new with the fork), and an untriaged CVE has no priority, so it
cannot be counted as P0/P1. After triage, ffmpeg-pengyos carries **3 P1s**, one
remote-reachable. "0 P0/P1 in our tree" is true of the triaged set and false of the
tree.

## Three findings that changed a decision

1. **hplip: 3.26.4 closes 2 of 9, not 9.** I told the operator NVD's range stops at
   3.26.4 — true for two CVEs, and I generalised it to all nine. Measured: 3.26.4 → 2,
   3.26.5 → 2, 3.26.6 → 9. Operator moved the pin to 3.26.6.
2. **ffmpeg: the fork closes 32 of 37, not 22 of 25.** My range arithmetic read NVD's
   `versionEndExcluding = "2025-01-13"` as a version tuple (2025.1.13), so 9.0.2
   sorted below it and I called CVE-2025-25468/-25469 closed. They are date-bounded
   and affected at 9.0.2. pkg-stats is the tool the ledger has to agree with.
3. **gst-libav cannot build against ffmpeg 9 at any release.** ffmpeg 9 removed
   `AV_CODEC_ID_V308/V408/V410`; gst-libav 1.24.13, 1.26.0 and 1.28.8 all name them.
   Upgrading gstreamer does not rescue the plugin. Guarded with the file's own
   `#if LIBAVCODEC_VERSION_MAJOR < 63` pattern, carried on our recipe.

## Loose ends, not fixed

1. **The plan still proposes the ffmpeg 6.1.5 patch set.** `security-ffmpeg-6.1.5`
   is the next batch. We ship 9.0.2, so those patches close nothing we build. The
   plan works on the stock tree and has no notion of "this component is forked".
2. **The nightly's carried counter cannot count a fork.** It counts CVEs that entered
   `state.json` as tracked matches; a fork's CVEs enter under the fork's package name,
   and the stock baseline stays open. The report's "20 closed by patches we carry"
   and the tool's "19" are both measuring something other than what ships.
3. **`carried` under-reports hand-carried fixes generally** — the podman fix is real
   (patch, ledger entry, declaration, pkg-stats suppression) and invisible to the
   counter, because NVD names no commit for it so it never became a tracked match.
4. **The gstreamer version decision is unmade.** The attribution says the fix is
   1.28.5+ across base/good/bad in lockstep, and that 8 of the 24 have no code fix to
   buy at any version. It also interacts with the ffmpeg fork through gst1-libav.
5. **CVE-2026-18295 / -18296 (MRF) are unattributed** — no advisory, no commit, and no
   MRF plugin in the monorepo's plugin directories.
